03 · Two ways to play in VR

Step up to the cabinet. Or step inside the mansion.

Immersive 3D VR

Stand right inside Sega's 1996 world at life size in full stereo 3D. Look around freely while the rail camera carries you through Curien Mansion.

  • A hook in the arcade's scenery code sends the entire surrounding area, so the mansion stays rendered behind and beside you.
  • Shots travel straight from the barrel of your red/blue arcade gun or moving-slide pistol, with recoil, flash and rumble.
  • Physical reloads replace the arcade's off-screen prompt with clear step-by-step hints: drop mag, insert mag, rack slide.
  • Hold the gun with both hands for steadier aim, or play controller-free with hand tracking on Quest and Vision Pro.

Arcade Cabinet Mode

Play the classic upright cabinet with restored marquee, control panel and side art — zero artificial camera motion.

  • Grab the cabinet with both grips to move it closer, rotate it, or scale it from tabletop to towering arcade height.
  • Choose your backdrop: the 3D Courtyard (generated locally from your ROM), Passthrough (your real room), or a Dark arcade space.
  • Shoot off the cabinet's screen for classic light-gun reloads, with the gun's cord connected right to the holster.
  • Hold your gun hand's thumbstick click at any moment to switch seamlessly between the cabinet and standing inside the game.

Also built in

Your reload style

Default physical magazine reloads (drop, insert, rack), quick point-down floor reloads, classic off-screen shots, or a B/Y button press — switchable anytime in the in-VR menu.

Hand tracking & in-VR menus

Full laser-pointed menus for gun model, left/right handedness, view, comfort, graphics and audio. Every preference saves automatically. No controllers required if you prefer bare hands.

Local 2-player on one screen

On Windows, Mac, phones, iPad and Apple TV, a second player can press Start on any other input device mid-run to drop in as Player 2 with the blue crosshair — just like dropping quarters at the cabinet.

DualSense & Xbox triggers

A DualSense right trigger builds real mechanical resistance and snaps on each shot; Xbox One/Series trigger motors kick on shots and reloads across Windows (SDL3), Mac, iOS and Apple TV.

HD or original 496×384

In flat mode, play in sharp native-resolution HD (4:3 with 4x MSAA) or toggle the authentic 496×384 arcade picture. Works with Sinden, GunCon 3 and Gun4IR light guns out of the box.

In-app updates

Meta Quest, Steam Frame and Android builds check GitHub Releases on launch and download, verify (SHA-256 + signature) and install updates from the SYSTEM tab while keeping your ROM and saves.

04 · Online Co-Op & Voice Chat

Two agents. Any two platforms. Frame for frame.

Team up as Rogan (red gun) and G (blue gun) over the internet or on your local network — no port forwarding, no accounts, and no platform walls.

  • True cross-platform lockstep: Every compiler builds the game with strict floating-point rules, so an ARM64 Quest 3 and an x64 Windows PC compute the exact same arcade RAM bit for bit, verified every 60 frames by checksum.
  • Internet rooms & zero-server LAN: Host a room with a shareable code, browse open games (each tagged with its platform: QUEST, PC, PCVR, MAC, IPHONE, APPLETV, VISIONPRO), hit Quick Match, or play directly on your local Wi-Fi with no server at all.
  • Drop into a game already running: Partners don't need to wait for a restart. The host packs a compressed snapshot (~180 KB LZMA) and streams it over so Player 2 joins mid-stage in 2–3 seconds.
  • Clear built-in voice chat: Opus audio with SpeexDSP echo cancellation, RNNoise background noise suppression, and optional push-to-talk (off-hand stick click in VR, T on keyboard).

Online rooms use zonesync.net by default (configurable in the ONLINE tab). Voice audio goes to your partner during play and is never recorded.

07 · Under the hood

Why it isn't an emulator

Instead of interpreting arcade machine code on the fly, the board's programs are statically recompiled ahead of time into native C++ — giving standalone headsets smooth 120 Hz head tracking and true 3D depth on top of the exact 1996 arcade logic.

Intel i960 Main CPU
The arcade game's entire program is statically recompiled ahead of time into native ARM64 / x64 C++.
Motorola 68000 & SCSP DSP
Sound CPU recompiled natively alongside a reimplemented SCSP audio chip for authentic arcade music and voice lines.
Fujitsu MB86235 (TGPx4)
Geometry coprocessor handled by an interpreter derived from MAME, keeping every transform and animation exact.
Geometrizer & 3D Renderer
One unified OpenGL ES 3.0 shader pipeline everywhere (native on Quest & Android, GL 3.3 on Windows, ANGLE on Metal on Apple) drawing true 3D stereo views.
Deterministic Crossplay
Compiled with strict floating-point flags across MSVC, NDK Clang and Apple Clang so every CPU calculates identical game RAM bit for bit.

08 · Download & Install

Ready in three quick steps

  1. Install the Quest APK

    Download the latest house-of-the-dead-vr.apk below and install it onto your Meta Quest 3 or 3S using SideQuest or adb install. Already on v0.4.1 or newer? Open the in-VR SYSTEM tab and press UPDATE directly in your headset.

  2. Copy your MAME ROM set

    Copy your own hotd.zip or hotd.7z (MAME Revision A) to your headset — for example into its Download folder over USB or from your own cloud storage.

  3. Launch, load & play

    Open House of the Dead VR from Unknown Sources, pull the trigger to pick your ROM set, and you're in. The app verifies it, stores its own copy, and offers to clean up the download file.

Download for your platform

Every download is built from the same commit on GitHub Releases and plays online with every other platform.

Meta Quest 3 / 3S (.apk) Windows PC & PC VR (.zip) Mac Apple Silicon (.zip) Android Phone / Tablet (.apk) iPhone & iPad (.ipa) Steam Frame (.apk)

Windows (flat & PC VR): Unzip anywhere and launch HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe. It starts in VR automatically when SteamVR, Quest Link or Virtual Desktop is active, or flat on your monitor otherwise (toggle anytime in SYSTEM, or pass --vr / --flat). Not code-signed: if SmartScreen prompts, click More info → Run anyway.

Mac (Apple silicon, experimental): Unzip, drag House of the Dead VR into Applications and open it. First launch: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

iPhone & iPad (experimental): Install the .ipa using SideStore (or TestFlight if invited), then pick your ROM file from the Files picker.

Apple TV & Apple Vision Pro (experimental): Available via TestFlight for invited testers. Vision Pro loads your ROM from Files; Apple TV shows a local web address and QR code so you can upload the ROM straight from a phone or laptop on the same Wi-Fi.

Bring your own game

None of the downloads contain any SEGA game code, artwork, models, sound or music. You need your own MAME arcade ROM set of The House of the Dead — hotd.zip or hotd.7z, Revision A (the older hotdo revision isn't compatible). Everything is loaded locally on your device. Please don't ask for ROMs.

09 · Status

Active development

The Meta Quest 3 version is the primary build, and Windows flat + PC VR builds are released alongside it. Mac, Android phone/tablet, iPhone/iPad, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro and Steam Frame builds are experimental and improving rapidly.

Every platform includes optional automatic crash reporting (which you can toggle off anytime in SYSTEM → Crash reports) so crashes reach the developer without hunting for log files. Hit a bug or want to share feedback? Open an issue on GitHub.

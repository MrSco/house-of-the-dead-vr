# House of the Dead VR 0.7.2

Water reflections, rain and other translucent effects now draw across the headset view when foveated rendering uses coarse shading near the edges. The fix was tested in-game on Quest 3. Thanks to StaticSymphony for reporting the missing layers. [Issue #7](https://github.com/MrSco/house-of-the-dead-vr/issues/7)

### Update

- On Quest, Steam Frame and Android, open **SYSTEM** and choose **UPDATE TO v0.7.2**.
- On Windows and Mac, download the new build below. Keep your existing `files` folder on Windows.
- On iPhone and iPad, you can install the IPA with SideStore. Invited testers can update iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro through TestFlight.

0.7.2 can play online with 0.4.x, 0.5.0, 0.6.x and earlier 0.7.x releases.

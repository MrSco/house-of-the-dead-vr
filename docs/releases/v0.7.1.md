# House of the Dead VR 0.7.1

The House of the Dead VR on Meta Quest 3: Sega's 1996 arcade game, statically recompiled to run natively on the headset, in full stereo 3D with a light gun in your hand, or at its arcade cabinet. Also for PC VR headsets, Windows, the Mac, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro and the Steam Frame, all playing online together.

**You need your own ROM set.** No download contains any ROM data, graphics or sound. Copy MAME's `hotd.zip` or `hotd.7z` (Revision A) to your device; the app asks for it on its first start.

### In this release

- **Your body stands straight when you do (headsets):** in 0.7.0 your agent's body could stand with bent knees while you stood straight, for minutes at a time. It was sized from the highest your head had been seen, and putting the headset on or looking down could read too high. It now settles on how you really stand, and recentering measures you again.
- **Racking the slide without knocking your controllers together:** your free hand no longer has to come to the back of the gun. Squeeze its grip anywhere along the top of the gun, from over the muzzle to about 20 cm behind it, and pull towards you. This is for controllers with tracking rings (Quest 2), which hit each other at the gun.

### Install


**Quest and Steam Frame:** open the menu's **SYSTEM** tab and choose **UPDATE TO v0.7.1**. From a version before 0.4.1, install `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.7.1.apk` with SideQuest or `adb install` over it; your saves are kept.

**Other platforms:**

- **Windows PC (screen or VR headset):** unzip `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.7.1-windows-x64.zip` over your old folder (your `files` folder is kept), or anywhere new, and run `HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe`. For VR, start your headset's PC link first. The app isn't code-signed: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac (Apple silicon), experimental:** unzip, move **House of the Dead VR** to Applications and open it; the first time, **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**.
- **Android phones and tablets, experimental** (64-bit, Android 10 or later): the in-app update, or install the APK.
- **iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro, experimental:** TestFlight, if you've been invited. iPhone and iPad can also use the IPA with SideStore.

### Please test

Racking from further back has been tried on a Quest 3 only: if you play with Quest 2 controllers, say whether your hands still knock together. The bodies have been played on a Quest 3 alone. Still to be seen by anyone: two headsets online together, a headset with a partner on a screen (does their agent aim where they aim?), and the bodies on a PC VR headset and Apple Vision Pro. If your own body gets in your way, **VIEW > Your body** switches it off. If something crashes, just start the app again: the report goes out by itself.

### Notes

- Online play: 0.7.1 plays with 0.4.x, 0.5.0, 0.6.x and 0.7.0 on every platform (the same online version), not with 0.3.0. A partner on 0.6.1 or earlier in a headset shows as a body too, its head placed from where their hands are.
- If you installed a test build signed differently, uninstall it first (Android refuses an update with a different signature). Back up the app's folder first if you want your saves.

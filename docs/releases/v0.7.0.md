# House of the Dead VR 0.7.0

The House of the Dead VR on Meta Quest 3: Sega's 1996 arcade game, statically recompiled to run natively on the headset, in full stereo 3D with a light gun in your hand, or at its arcade cabinet. Also for PC VR headsets, Windows, the Mac, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro and the Steam Frame, all playing online together.

**You need your own ROM set.** No download contains any ROM data, graphics or sound. Copy MAME's `hotd.zip` or `hotd.7z` (Revision A) to your device; the app asks for it on its first start.

### In this release

- **You and your partner are the game's agents (headsets):** look down and you have a body: Rogan if you are player 1, G if you are player 2, with arms, chest and legs that follow your head and hands. Online, your partner stands beside you the same way, looking where they look and aiming where they aim. The figures are the game's own, read from your ROM set on your device the first time the app starts (a few seconds in; until then you see hands and guns as before).
- **The agents' own hands and pistol:** a body holds the game's own pistol in the game's own hand. It kicks when you fire, moves as you rack the slide, flashes at the muzzle, and takes a magazine in its grip.
- **Partners on a screen show too:** someone playing on Windows, a Mac, a phone, a tablet or a TV has no head or hands to track, so their agent stands beside you, aims where their gun aims on the screen and fires when they fire.
- **Three new settings:**
  - **VIEW > Your body** (ON / OFF): your agent's body, or hands and gun alone.
  - **VIEW > Partner** (BODY / HANDS): your partner as the whole agent, or as hands and gun alone, drawn the way they have set their own.
  - **HANDS > Your hands** (MODERN / ORIGINAL / NONE), for when Your body is OFF: the detailed hands with the gun you chose, the game's own hands and pistol, or just your gun.
- Nothing changes on flat screens: there is no view beside you there.

### Install

**Quest and Steam Frame:** open the menu's **SYSTEM** tab and choose **UPDATE TO v0.7.0**. From a version before 0.4.1, install `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.7.0.apk` with SideQuest or `adb install` over it; your saves are kept.

**Other platforms:**

- **Windows PC (screen or VR headset):** unzip `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.7.0-windows-x64.zip` over your old folder (your `files` folder is kept), or anywhere new, and run `HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe`. For VR, start your headset's PC link first. The app isn't code-signed: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac (Apple silicon), experimental:** unzip, move **House of the Dead VR** to Applications and open it; the first time, **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**.
- **Android phones and tablets, experimental** (64-bit, Android 10 or later): the in-app update, or install the APK.
- **iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro, experimental:** TestFlight, if you've been invited. iPhone and iPad can also use the IPA with SideStore.

### Please test

The bodies have been played on a Quest 3 alone. Still to be seen by anyone: two headsets online together, a headset with a partner on a screen (does their agent aim where they aim?), and the bodies on a PC VR headset and Apple Vision Pro. If your own body gets in your way, **VIEW > Your body** switches it off. If something crashes, just start the app again: the report goes out by itself.

### Notes

- Online play: 0.7.0 plays with 0.4.x, 0.5.0 and 0.6.x on every platform (the same online version), not with 0.3.0. A partner on 0.6.1 or earlier in a headset shows as a body too, its head placed from where their hands are.
- If you installed a test build signed differently, uninstall it first (Android refuses an update with a different signature). Back up the app's folder first if you want your saves.

# House of the Dead VR 0.6.1

The House of the Dead VR on Meta Quest 3: Sega's 1996 arcade game, statically recompiled to run natively on the headset, in full stereo 3D with a light gun in your hand, or at its arcade cabinet. Also for PC VR headsets, Windows, the Mac, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro and the Steam Frame, all playing online together.

**You need your own ROM set.** No download contains any ROM data, graphics or sound. Copy MAME's `hotd.zip` or `hotd.7z` (Revision A) to your device; the app asks for it on its first start.

### In this release

- **Crash reports on every platform:** if the app crashes, it sends a report the next time it starts, so problems testers hit reach the developer without anyone having to dig out log files. It now works on the Quest, Steam Frame, Windows (screen and PC VR), the Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Vision Pro and Android phones. A report holds the crash, the end of the app's log, the build, your settings and the device model; no account, name or address (see [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/MrSco/house-of-the-dead-vr/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)). **Crash reports** in the menu's **SYSTEM** tab switches it off.
- **Cleaner builds:** the downloads no longer contain file paths from the machine they were built on, and every release is now checked for that before it's published. Older downloads have been removed from their releases; use this one.
- Otherwise the same game as 0.6.0: local two-player on flat screens, rumble, touch haptics and trigger feedback.

### Install

**Quest and Steam Frame:** open the menu's **SYSTEM** tab and choose **UPDATE TO v0.6.1**. From a version before 0.4.1, install `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.6.1.apk` with SideQuest or `adb install` over it; your saves are kept.

**Other platforms:**

- **Windows PC (screen or VR headset):** unzip `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.6.1-windows-x64.zip` over your old folder (your `files` folder is kept), or anywhere new, and run `HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe`. For VR, start your headset's PC link first. The app isn't code-signed: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac (Apple silicon), experimental:** unzip, move **House of the Dead VR** to Applications and open it; the first time, **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**.
- **Android phones and tablets, experimental** (64-bit, Android 10 or later): the in-app update, or install the APK.
- **iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro, experimental:** TestFlight, if you've been invited. iPhone and iPad can also use the IPA with SideStore.

### Please test

The same list as 0.6.0: two players on one screen with real pads, rumble and trigger feedback on Xbox and DualSense pads, phone haptics, the hit rumble in the headsets, and online play between platforms. If something crashes, just start the app again: the report goes out by itself.

### Notes

- Online play: 0.6.1 plays with 0.4.x, 0.5.0 and 0.6.0 on every platform (the same online version), not with 0.3.0.
- If you installed a test build signed differently, uninstall it first (Android refuses an update with a different signature). Back up the app's folder first if you want your saves.

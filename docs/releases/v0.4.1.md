# House of the Dead VR 0.4.1

The House of the Dead VR on Meta Quest 3: Sega's 1996 arcade game, statically recompiled to run natively on the headset, in full stereo 3D with a light gun in your hand, or at its arcade cabinet. Also for Windows, the Mac, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, and the Steam Frame, all playing online together.

**You need your own ROM set.** No download contains any ROM data, graphics or sound. Copy MAME's `hotd.zip` or `hotd.7z` (Revision A) to your device; the app asks for it on its first start.

### In this release

- **The app updates itself (Quest and Steam Frame).** It looks for a new release each time it starts. The menu's **SYSTEM** tab shows it (with a gold dot on the tab) beside CHANGE ROM SET: **UPDATE TO ...** downloads it, checks it and hands it to the headset's installer for you to confirm. Your ROM set, settings and saves are kept. The game closes once it's installed; start it again from the Library. The first update asks you to allow **Install unknown apps** for the app.
- **TEST BUILDS** on the same tab: switch it on to be offered test builds (pre-releases) before a release. They may have bugs; switch it off to get releases only.
- Nothing else changed from 0.4.0: the game and online play are the same.

### Install

**Quest:** install `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.4.1.apk` with SideQuest or `adb install`, over 0.4.0 or 0.3.0 (your saves are kept). 0.4.0 doesn't have the updater yet, so this one is by hand; later releases come through the SYSTEM tab.

**Other platforms:**

- **Windows PC:** unzip `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.4.1-windows-x64.zip` anywhere and run `HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe` (or put `hotd.zip` in `HOTDVR/files/arcade` first). The mouse is the gun; Xbox, PlayStation and Switch pads work too, and light guns that act as a mouse (Sinden, GunCon 3, Gun4IR). Space or Enter put in coins and press Start, Esc opens the menu, F11 or Alt+Enter goes full screen. The app isn't code-signed: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. To update, unzip the new one over the old folder (your `files` folder is kept).
- **Mac (Apple silicon), experimental:** unzip, move **House of the Dead VR** to Applications and open it. It isn't notarized: the first time, macOS refuses it; open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- **Android phones and tablets, experimental** (64-bit, Android 10 or later): tap the picture to shoot, a second finger reloads; pads and the phone's gyroscope can aim too. Tested in an emulator only.
- **iPhone and iPad, experimental:** install the IPA with SideStore, which signs it with your Apple ID. Tested in the simulator only.
- **Steam Frame, experimental:** the Quest app for Valve's headset, with the same updater. It builds, but nobody has run it on a Frame yet.

### Notes

- Online play: 0.4.1 plays with 0.4.0 on every platform (the same online version), not with 0.3.0.
- If you installed a test build signed differently, uninstall it first (Android refuses an update with a different signature). Back up the app's folder first if you want your saves.
- Tested on Quest 3. The Quest 3S should work but hasn't been tested.

# House of the Dead VR 0.4.2

The House of the Dead VR on Meta Quest 3: Sega's 1996 arcade game, statically recompiled to run natively on the headset, in full stereo 3D with a light gun in your hand, or at its arcade cabinet. Also for Windows, the Mac, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, and the Steam Frame, all playing online together.

**You need your own ROM set.** No download contains any ROM data, graphics or sound. Copy MAME's `hotd.zip` or `hotd.7z` (Revision A) to your device; the app asks for it on its first start.

### In this release

- **An app icon everywhere:** the Quest and Steam Frame library, Android's launcher, the Windows exe and taskbar, the Mac's Dock and the iPhone and iPad home screen now show the House of the Dead VR artwork instead of a blank icon.
- **iPhone and iPad on TestFlight:** besides the IPA for SideStore, the iPhone and iPad app now goes to Apple's TestFlight, signed for the App Store. Testers are invited by the developer.
- **Privacy:** the project now has a privacy policy ([PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/MrSco/house-of-the-dead-vr/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)): no accounts, no ads, no tracking; what online play, voice chat, crash reports and the update check send.
- Nothing else changed: the game and online play are the same as 0.4.1.

### Install

**Quest and Steam Frame:** on 0.4.1, open the menu's **SYSTEM** tab and choose **UPDATE TO v0.4.2** (the first update asks you to allow **Install unknown apps**). From an older version, install `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.4.2.apk` with SideQuest or `adb install` over it; your saves are kept.

**Other platforms:**

- **Windows PC:** unzip `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.4.2-windows-x64.zip` anywhere and run `HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe` (or put `hotd.zip` in `HOTDVR/files/arcade` first). The mouse is the gun; Xbox, PlayStation and Switch pads work too, and light guns that act as a mouse (Sinden, GunCon 3, Gun4IR). Space or Enter put in coins and press Start, Esc opens the menu, F11 or Alt+Enter goes full screen. The app isn't code-signed: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. To update, unzip the new one over the old folder (your `files` folder is kept).
- **Mac (Apple silicon), experimental:** unzip, move **House of the Dead VR** to Applications and open it. It isn't notarized: the first time, macOS refuses it; open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- **Android phones and tablets, experimental** (64-bit, Android 10 or later): tap the picture to shoot, a second finger reloads; pads and the phone's gyroscope can aim too. Tested in an emulator only.
- **iPhone and iPad, experimental:** TestFlight, if you've been invited, or the IPA with SideStore, which signs it with your Apple ID. Tested in the simulator only.
- **Steam Frame, experimental:** the Quest app for Valve's headset, with the same updater. It builds, but nobody has run it on a Frame yet.

### Notes

- Online play: 0.4.2 plays with 0.4.0 and 0.4.1 on every platform (the same online version), not with 0.3.0.
- If you installed a test build signed differently, uninstall it first (Android refuses an update with a different signature). Back up the app's folder first if you want your saves.
- Tested on Quest 3. The Quest 3S should work but hasn't been tested.

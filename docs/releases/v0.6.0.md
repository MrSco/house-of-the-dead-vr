# House of the Dead VR 0.6.0

The House of the Dead VR on Meta Quest 3: Sega's 1996 arcade game, statically recompiled to run natively on the headset, in full stereo 3D with a light gun in your hand, or at its arcade cabinet. Also for PC VR headsets, Windows, the Mac, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro and the Steam Frame, all playing online together.

**You need your own ROM set.** No download contains any ROM data, graphics or sound. Copy MAME's `hotd.zip` or `hotd.7z` (Revision A) to your device; the app asks for it on its first start.

### In this release

- **Two players on one screen:** on Windows, the Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Android, a second player joins at any time, as on the arcade cabinet: press **Start** (A or Start on a pad; on Apple TV, hold Play/Pause on the Siri Remote) on a second controller while player 1 is in a stage. That controller becomes player 2's gun, with the blue crosshair. Playing alone, every controller (mouse and keyboard, touch, the Siri Remote, any pad) still controls player 1, so connecting a pad never takes anything over; on the continue screen, Start always continues player 1. A disconnected pad comes back to its player. Local two-player is offline only: online, the second gun is your partner's.
- **Rumble and haptics everywhere:** a kick on each shot, a softer pulse on a reload and a strong rumble when you're hit, for each player on their own controller.
  - **Pads** on every flat app (Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Android).
  - **Touch:** iPhone and Android phones tap and buzz in your hand.
  - **Headsets:** the Quest, Steam Frame, PC VR and Vision Pro's Sense controllers now rumble when you're hit too.
- **Trigger feedback:** a PS5 DualSense's right trigger resists and snaps like a gun's while you play, and buzzes on each shot; an Xbox One or Series pad's trigger motors buzz on shots and reloads (Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV). **Rumble** and **Trigger feedback** are in the menu's **GAME** tab, both on; Rumble OFF silences both. On Windows and the Mac, **Display** moved to the **SYSTEM** tab to make room.
- **Fixes:**
  - On the headsets, the reload hints, the magazine drop and the empty-gun click now work when you play online as player 2.
  - Player 2's lives and bullets are read correctly.
  - The Apple TV crosshair has its black outline.

### Install

**Quest and Steam Frame:** open the menu's **SYSTEM** tab and choose **UPDATE TO v0.6.0**. From a version before 0.4.1, install `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.6.0.apk` with SideQuest or `adb install` over it; your saves are kept.

**Other platforms:**

- **Windows PC (screen or VR headset):** unzip `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.6.0-windows-x64.zip` over your old folder (your `files` folder is kept), or anywhere new, and run `HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe`. For VR, start your headset's PC link first. The app isn't code-signed: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac (Apple silicon), experimental:** unzip, move **House of the Dead VR** to Applications and open it; the first time, **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**.
- **Android phones and tablets, experimental** (64-bit, Android 10 or later): the in-app update, or install the APK.
- **iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro, experimental:** TestFlight, if you've been invited. iPhone and iPad can also use the IPA with SideStore.

### Please test

This release is mostly for testers. The things nobody has tried on real hardware yet:

- **Two players on one screen** with two real pads, on each platform: joining, continuing, a pad disconnecting and reconnecting, and a light gun or the mouse beside a pad.
- **Rumble and trigger feedback** on real Xbox and DualSense pads: does it feel right, and is a DualSense's trigger ever left stiff after pausing, opening the menu, quitting or disconnecting?
- **Phones:** the touch haptics on an iPhone and an Android phone.
- **Headsets:** the hit rumble on the Quest, a PC headset and the Vision Pro's Sense controllers.
- **Online play** between different platforms: the platform tag in the game browser, joining by code, quick match and the local network.

### Notes

- Online play: 0.6.0 plays with 0.4.x and 0.5.0 on every platform (the same online version), not with 0.3.0.
- Android has no API for a pad's triggers, so Android pads rumble but get no trigger feedback; the same goes for Windows without `SDL3.dll` (XInput only).
- If you installed a test build signed differently, uninstall it first (Android refuses an update with a different signature). Back up the app's folder first if you want your saves.

# House of the Dead VR 0.8.1

The House of the Dead VR on Meta Quest 3: Sega's 1996 arcade game, statically recompiled to run natively on the headset, in full stereo 3D with a light gun in your hand, or at its arcade cabinet. Also for PC VR headsets, Windows, the Mac, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro and the Steam Frame, all playing online together.

**You need your own ROM set.** No download contains any ROM data, graphics or sound. Copy MAME's `hotd.zip` or `hotd.7z` (Revision A) to your device; the app asks for it on its first start.

### In this release

- **Quitting during an online game no longer crashes:** on an iPhone playing as player 2, choosing **QUIT** in the menu could crash the app as it closed. The game and the app both closed the sound at the same moment, and an online partner's game notices the quit at once, so the two often collided. The sound now closes once.
- **Quitting keeps your last scores and settings:** the game wrote its high scores and its own Options page's settings every few seconds, so a score set just before you quit could be lost. It now writes them once more as it ends, and the app waits for that before it closes (iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac, Windows and Android).

### Install

**Quest and Steam Frame:** open the menu's **SYSTEM** tab and choose **UPDATE TO v0.8.1**. From a version before 0.4.1, install `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.8.1.apk` with SideQuest or `adb install` over it; your saves are kept.

**Other platforms:**

- **Windows PC (screen or VR headset):** unzip `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.8.1-windows-x64.zip` over your old folder (your `files` folder is kept), or anywhere new, and run `HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe`. For VR, start your headset's PC link first. The app isn't code-signed: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac (Apple silicon), experimental:** unzip, move **House of the Dead VR** to Applications and open it; the first time, **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**.
- **Android phones and tablets, experimental** (64-bit, Android 10 or later): the in-app update, or install the APK.
- **iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro, experimental:** TestFlight, if you've been invited (with iCloud sync, new in 0.8.0). iPhone and iPad can also use the IPA with SideStore.

### Notes

- Online play: 0.8.1 plays with 0.4.x, 0.5.0, 0.6.x, 0.7.x and 0.8.0 on every platform (the same online version).

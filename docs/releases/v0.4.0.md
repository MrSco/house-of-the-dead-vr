# House of the Dead VR 0.4.0

The House of the Dead VR on Meta Quest 3: Sega's 1996 arcade game, statically recompiled to run natively on the headset, in full stereo 3D with a light gun in your hand, or at its arcade cabinet.

**You need your own ROM set.** This release contains no ROM data, graphics or sound. Copy MAME's `hotd.zip` or `hotd.7z` (Revision A) to the headset; the app asks for it on its first start.

### Install

1. Install `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.4.0.apk` with SideQuest or `adb install`. It updates 0.3.0 in place; your settings and saves are kept.
2. Copy your ROM set to the headset (for example into Download).
3. Start **House of the Dead VR** from Unknown Sources, pull the trigger and choose the ROM set.

The menu's **CONTROLS** button lists every control. Hold the gun hand's thumbstick click to switch between standing in the game and the cabinet.

### In this release

- **Cleaner 3D in the immersive view:** the sky's mountain band no longer breaks up as you look around; the ground at your feet no longer flickers; floors no longer go missing under you; zombies and bodies stay on top of the floors and walls they stand on, as the arcade draws them.
- **Brightness setting** (GRAPHICS): lifts the game's darkest shades, which came out black. The shadows' colours are also now exact.
- **RESTART** in the menu takes the game back to its start, as if the cabinet had just been switched on. Your settings and high scores are kept. It replaces the GAME MENU button, which did nothing.
- **Reload gestures talk you through it:** with gesture reloading on, the announcer says "Drop the mag!", "Insert a magazine!" or "Rack the slide!" for the step you're on. Shots near the edges of the picture no longer count as reloads.
- **Comfort:** the turn vignette is one ring centred where you look, visible on LOW. It also reacts to fast camera moves, indoors as well as out. The horizon levelling works indoors too.
- **Game over** plays on a screen in the dark ahead of you, instead of jumping to the cabinet's room.
- **Online:** a friend can join a game already in progress and picks up exactly where you are.
- **The cabinet:** a stray piece of the gun cord is gone, and the front panel over the holsters is a uniform dark grey.

### Other platforms

The same game, built from the same code, for screens too. They all play online with each other and with the Quest
(the same version on every side: 0.4.0). Each one asks for your ROM set at its first start.

- **Windows PC:** unzip `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.4.0-windows-x64.zip` anywhere and run `HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe` (or put
  `hotd.zip` in `HOTDVR/files/arcade` first). The mouse is the gun; Xbox, PlayStation and Switch pads work too, and
  light guns that act as a mouse (Sinden, GunCon 3, Gun4IR). Space or Enter put in coins and press Start, Esc opens the menu, F11 or Alt+Enter goes full screen. The app isn't code-signed:
  if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac (Apple silicon), experimental:** unzip, move **House of the Dead VR** to Applications and open it. It isn't
  notarized: the first time, macOS refuses it; open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open
  Anyway**.
- **Android phones and tablets, experimental** (64-bit, Android 10 or later): tap the picture to shoot, a second finger
  reloads; pads and the phone's gyroscope can aim too. Tested in an emulator only.
- **iPhone and iPad, experimental:** install the IPA with SideStore, which signs it with your Apple ID. Tested in the
  simulator only.
- **Steam Frame, experimental:** the Quest app for Valve's headset. It builds, but nobody has run it on a Frame yet.

### Notes

- Online play needs the same version on both headsets: 0.4.0 cannot play with 0.3.0.
- If you installed a test build signed differently, uninstall it first (Android refuses an update with a different signature). Back up the app's folder first if you want your saves.
- Tested on Quest 3. The Quest 3S should work but hasn't been tested.

# House of the Dead VR 0.5.0

The House of the Dead VR on Meta Quest 3: Sega's 1996 arcade game, statically recompiled to run natively on the headset, in full stereo 3D with a light gun in your hand, or at its arcade cabinet. Also for PC VR headsets, Windows, the Mac, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro and the Steam Frame, all playing online together.

**You need your own ROM set.** No download contains any ROM data, graphics or sound. Copy MAME's `hotd.zip` or `hotd.7z` (Revision A) to your device; the app asks for it on its first start.

### In this release

- **PC VR:** `HOTDVR.exe` now plays in a VR headset through OpenXR (SteamVR, Quest Link or Air Link, Virtual Desktop), with the same immersive and cabinet views as the Quest. It starts in VR when a headset is connected and on the screen otherwise; **PLAY IN VR** / **PLAY ON THE SCREEN** in the menu's **SYSTEM** tab switches and remembers the choice (`--vr` and `--flat` force one). Under SteamVR, which keeps the left menu button for its dashboard, double-click a thumbstick to open the game's menu. Quest Touch, Index, Vive and Windows Mixed Reality controllers are supported.
- **Apple TV, experimental:** the game on the TV with the Siri Remote as the gun. Swipe the touch surface to aim (like a mouse), click to shoot, tap Play/Pause to reload, hold it for coins and Start, and Back opens the menu. On the newer remote the centre click shoots and the ring's edges reload. Game pads work too. There's no Files app on a TV, so the app shows a web address and a QR code: open it on a phone or computer on the same network and send it your `hotd.zip`. On the Apple TV HD the picture starts as **Original** to keep the game at full speed.
- **Apple Vision Pro, experimental:** the VR game in a full immersive space, and the cabinet view. Aim with your hand and pinch to shoot, or use PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers. The ROM set comes from the Files picker. Tested in the visionOS simulator only.
- **Each game's platform in the online browser:** rooms and LAN games now show what their host plays on (QUEST, PC, PCVR, APPLETV and so on) beside the name. 0.4.x players see it as part of the room's name and can still join.
- **Smoother sound on slower devices:** when a phone, tablet or TV can't keep up with the busiest scenes, the game now skips drawing a frame (never two in a row) instead of letting the sound cut out.
- **Fixes:** the Windows app restarts into the right folder when started with a relative `--files` path; a setting's reset button goes back to that device's own default.
- Under the hood, the Quest, PC VR and Vision Pro now share one VR code base. Nothing should look different on the Quest.

### Install

**Quest and Steam Frame:** on 0.4.1 or 0.4.2, open the menu's **SYSTEM** tab and choose **UPDATE TO v0.5.0**. From an older version, install `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.5.0.apk` with SideQuest or `adb install` over it; your saves are kept.

**Other platforms:**

- **Windows PC (screen or VR headset):** unzip `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.5.0-windows-x64.zip` anywhere and run `HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe` (or put `hotd.zip` in `HOTDVR/files/arcade` first). For VR, start your headset's PC link (SteamVR, Quest Link, Virtual Desktop) first. On the screen, the mouse is the gun; Xbox, PlayStation and Switch pads work too, and light guns that act as a mouse (Sinden, GunCon 3, Gun4IR). Space or Enter put in coins and press Start, Esc opens the menu, F11 or Alt+Enter goes full screen. The app isn't code-signed: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. To update, unzip the new one over the old folder (your `files` folder is kept).
- **Mac (Apple silicon), experimental:** unzip, move **House of the Dead VR** to Applications and open it. It isn't notarized: the first time, macOS refuses it; open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- **Android phones and tablets, experimental** (64-bit, Android 10 or later): tap the picture to shoot, a second finger reloads; pads and the phone's gyroscope can aim too.
- **iPhone and iPad, experimental:** TestFlight, if you've been invited, or the IPA with SideStore, which signs it with your Apple ID.
- **Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro, experimental:** TestFlight, if you've been invited (SideStore can't install on either).
- **Steam Frame, experimental:** the Quest app for Valve's headset, with the same updater. It builds, but nobody has run it on a Frame yet.

### Notes

- Online play: 0.5.0 plays with 0.4.0, 0.4.1 and 0.4.2 on every platform (the same online version), not with 0.3.0.
- Apple TV: tvOS may clear an app's storage when it runs short of space. Your settings and saves are kept anyway; the ROM set may have to be sent again, and the app says so when it does. Voice chat on Apple TV only listens (there's no microphone).
- If you installed a test build signed differently, uninstall it first (Android refuses an update with a different signature). Back up the app's folder first if you want your saves.
- Tested on Quest 3, Apple TV 4K and Apple TV HD (both Siri Remote generations), and with SteamVR's test driver for PC VR. The Quest 3S should work but hasn't been tested.

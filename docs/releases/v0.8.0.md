# House of the Dead VR 0.8.0

The House of the Dead VR on Meta Quest 3: Sega's 1996 arcade game, statically recompiled to run natively on the headset, in full stereo 3D with a light gun in your hand, or at its arcade cabinet. Also for PC VR headsets, Windows, the Mac, Android phones, iPhone and iPad, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro and the Steam Frame, all playing online together.

**You need your own ROM set.** No download contains any ROM data, graphics or sound. Copy MAME's `hotd.zip` or `hotd.7z` (Revision A) to your device; the app asks for it on its first start.

### In this release

- **iCloud sync (iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro, from TestFlight):** your settings, high scores and ROM set follow you to your other Apple devices on the same iCloud account. Copy the ROM set to one device and the others download it from iCloud (the set goes up on Wi-Fi). A device joining for the first time takes the settings and saves already in iCloud, and keeps its own saves in a backup folder. **SYSTEM > iCloud sync** switches it off for a device, and the same row shows what it is doing.
- **A DualSense trigger that feels like a gun's:** the right trigger resists, then snaps, and fires only once you pull through the snap, not at the first touch. The left trigger clicks lightly for a reload. Each shot still buzzes. **Trigger feedback** switches the triggers off and keeps the rumble. An Xbox pad's trigger motors buzz as before. (Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.)
- **Music and Effects work:** the **SYSTEM > Music** and **Effects** levels never changed anything before. Music now sets the soundtrack alone and Effects everything else (shots, hits, the zombies and the game's voices). Game volume still sets them all.

### Install

**Quest and Steam Frame:** open the menu's **SYSTEM** tab and choose **UPDATE TO v0.8.0**. From a version before 0.4.1, install `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.8.0.apk` with SideQuest or `adb install` over it; your saves are kept.

**Other platforms:**

- **Windows PC (screen or VR headset):** unzip `house-of-the-dead-vr-0.8.0-windows-x64.zip` over your old folder (your `files` folder is kept), or anywhere new, and run `HOTDVR/HOTDVR.exe`. For VR, start your headset's PC link first. The app isn't code-signed: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac (Apple silicon), experimental:** unzip, move **House of the Dead VR** to Applications and open it; the first time, **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**.
- **Android phones and tablets, experimental** (64-bit, Android 10 or later): the in-app update, or install the APK.
- **iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro, experimental:** TestFlight, if you've been invited. iPhone and iPad can also use the IPA with SideStore (without iCloud sync: that needs the TestFlight app's signing).

### Please test

iCloud sync has been tried on an iPhone and an Apple TV together. Still to be seen: an iPad and Apple Vision Pro joining, and a device that already has its own settings joining one that is set up. The DualSense triggers have been checked on Windows; on a Mac, iPhone, iPad or Apple TV, does the right trigger snap before it fires? If something crashes, just start the app again: the report goes out by itself.

### Notes

- Online play: 0.8.0 plays with 0.4.x, 0.5.0, 0.6.x and 0.7.x on every platform (the same online version).
- The Music and Effects levels change only your own speakers: two players with different levels still play the same game online.
